# Naval Ravikant's Wealth and Leverage Playbook

**An unofficial, fully sourced playbook and AI skill that runs Naval Ravikant's wealth method (specific
knowledge, accountability, leverage, judgement, equity and long-term games) on your career, business or next
big decision. Built only from his own recorded words.**

Naval Ravikant co-founded AngelList and, earlier, Epinions, and has been an early investor in companies
including Twitter. On 31 May 2018 he posted a 40-tweet thread, *How to Get Rich (without getting lucky)*, then
spent 37 podcast episodes in 2019 explaining it with Babak Nivi. This repo turns the thread, the podcast, 48 of
his standalone X posts and his other first-party posts, episodes and interviews, from 2005 to 2026, into a method you can run on your own work
this week.

> "Seek wealth, not money or status. Wealth is having assets that earn while you sleep."
> Naval Ravikant, How to Get Rich thread, [TWEET18 #2](https://x.com/naval/status/1002103497725173760) (31 May 2018)

> ⚠️ **Unofficial.** Not written, reviewed or endorsed by Naval Ravikant, AngelList or Eric Jorgenson. It's a
> structured guide in our own words, with short credited quotes and a link to every source. It covers his ideas
> on wealth, startups, investing, learning, decisions and happiness as they relate to building: no politics, no
> crypto advocacy, no personal life. **Not financial, investment, legal or tax advice.**

## How to use this

### 1. Run it as an AI skill (10 minutes)

**Install** into your agent's skills folder. For Claude Code:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/01ayushgarg/naval-ravikant-wealth-leverage-playbook ~/.claude/skills/naval-ravikant-wealth-leverage-playbook
```

If your agent doesn't read skill folders, paste `SKILL.md` into the chat and attach the chapters it asks for.

**Then ask it to run a session.** Copy and fill in:

```text
Run Naval's wealth and leverage playbook on my work.

What I do, and how I'm paid (hourly, salary, equity, revenue):
What I own that earns without my time:
What people come to me for, unprompted:
Where my week goes, in hours:
Who I work with, and how long we've worked together:
The decision or deal on my desk (if any):
My context (country, industry, months of savings):
```

**You get back:** where you are (renting time or owning), your likely specific knowledge and how we know, what
you can put your name on, a leverage table (labor, capital, code, media) with the next move in each, your
effective and aspirational hourly rate, a verdict on the decision on your desk, three actions for this month, one
number to watch, and where his advice may not fit you. Every point cites the tweet, episode or interview it comes
from.

**Other things you can ask:**
- *What's my specific knowledge?*
- *I'm paid a salary. Is this equity offer worth the pay cut?*
- *What permissionless leverage could I start this week?*
- *Should I sign this deal, or walk away?*
- *Which of these two term sheets should I take?*
- *Is this person the right co-founder or first hire?*
- *What repeated work could I hand to AI?*
- *Write an angel memo on this deal.*

### 2. Use the templates (30 minutes, no AI)

1. [Specific knowledge finder](templates/01-specific-knowledge-finder.md) once, then again in three months.
2. [Leverage audit](templates/02-leverage-audit.md) once a quarter, with the [AI leverage plan](templates/11-ai-leverage-plan.md).
3. [Hourly rate and time audit](templates/03-hourly-rate-and-time-audit.md) every week.
4. [Decision filter](templates/06-decision-filter.md) whenever a choice locks you in for years.
5. [Deal and fundraise check](templates/09-deal-and-fundraise-check.md) before signing any contract or term sheet.
6. [Equity offer check](templates/10-equity-offer-check.md) before accepting options.
7. [Long-term partner check](templates/04-long-term-partner-check.md) and [hiring scorecard](templates/08-hiring-scorecard.md) before any co-founder, key hire or partner.
8. [Productize yourself plan](templates/05-productize-yourself-plan.md) for the next 12 weeks.
9. [Angel investment memo](templates/07-angel-investment-memo.md) only if you invest.

He says he doesn't use checklists for his own decisions [TKP-18]. The templates are our aids; drop any that slow
you down.

### 3. Read it

Start with chapter 05 if all your income stops when you stop, 02 if you feel replaceable, 03 or 17 if your output
is capped by your hours, 10 or 15 if you're stuck on a decision or deal, 16 if you're raising, 12 if you're about
to write a check, and 18 if you're outside tech.

**His own caveat:** "Even in this podcast, you should examine everything. If something doesn't feel true to you,
put it down." [RICH-34] Use this to sharpen your judgement, not to replace it.

## How it stays honest

- **First-party only:** his 2018 thread, 48 of his standalone X posts, his nav.al posts and podcasts (2005 to 2026), his Tim Ferriss Show and
  Knowledge Project transcripts, and Venture Hacks posts. No quote sites, no summaries of summaries.
- **Quotes matched by script before publishing:** every quote of 20 characters or more was matched against a
  plain-text capture of the source it cites (normalising only quote marks, dashes and whitespace), and for
  interviews against his own turns only. Shorter fragments were checked by hand. The captures and the script
  aren't shipped, because the texts are the publishers'; `SOURCES.md` explains how to repeat the check.
- **Short quotes:** none over 60 words, and quoting from any one source kept to about a tenth of it. The 2018
  thread is mostly paraphrased, with a link to every tweet. Each standalone post is linked and dated; their dates
  were checked against the post IDs.
- **Every claim cited** with a source ID, which maps to a dated link in [`SOURCES.md`](SOURCES.md).
- **Other speakers labelled:** lines from Babak Nivi, Tim Ferriss or Shane Parrish are marked as theirs.
- **Edited transcripts flagged:** nav.al edits its podcast transcripts for clarity, and we say so.
- **The Almanack is labelled:** it's compiled by Eric Jorgenson, so we lead with primary sources. The lines once
  cited only to it (*If you can't decide, the answer is no*, the short-term-pain tie-breaker and the
  one-hour-a-day reading line) now cite his original X posts first.
- **Not in the corpus, not quoted:** his Joe Rogan interview has no official transcript, so nothing from it
  appears here.
- **Our own reading is marked:** steps, thresholds, mechanics, worked numbers and templates are labelled as ours,
  never presented as his words.
- **Limits stated:** chapter 18 covers survivorship, place, starting points and dated material.
