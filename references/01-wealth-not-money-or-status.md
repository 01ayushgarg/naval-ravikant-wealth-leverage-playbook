## Three different things

> "Seek wealth, not money or status. Wealth is having assets that earn while you sleep."
> [TWEET18 #2](https://x.com/naval/status/1002103497725173760)

> "Money is how we transfer time and wealth. Status is your place in the social hierarchy."
> [TWEET18 #2](https://x.com/naval/status/1002103497725173760)

| | What it is, in his words | Game |
|---|---|---|
| **Wealth** | "Wealth is the factory, the robots, that's cranking out things. Wealth is the computer program that's running at night, that's serving other customers." [RICH-01] | Positive sum |
| **Money** | "Money is social credits. It is the ability to have credits and debits of other people's time." [RICH-01] | A record of value already created |
| **Status** | "Status, on the other hand, is a zero-sum game." [RICH-01] | Zero sum |

> "Wealth is a very positive sum game. We create things together." [RICH-01]

> "The problem is that to win at a status game, you have to put somebody else down." [RICH-01]

**The move:** before any plan, sort what you're chasing into the three columns. Only the first compounds while
you sleep.

## What wealth is for

> "So, the purpose of wealth is freedom. It's nothing more than that." [RICH-01]

> "To me the ultimate purpose of money is so you don't have to be in a specific place, at a specific time,
> doing anything you don't want to do." [RICH-35]

> "Because money is not going to solve all of your problems, but it's going to solve all of your money
> problems." [RICH-01]

In 2020 he put it the same way: "So to me, the purpose of money is freedom." [TF-473]

## Make it ethically, or it eludes you

> "Understand that ethical wealth creation is possible. If you secretly despise wealth, it will elude you."
> [TWEET18 #3](https://x.com/naval/status/1002103559276478464)

> "Ignore people playing status games. They gain status by attacking people playing wealth creation games."
> [TWEET18 #4](https://x.com/naval/status/1002103627387813888)

> "What I am focused on is true wealth creation. It's not about taking money." [RICH-02]

## Where wealth comes from

> "You will get rich by giving society what it wants but does not yet know how to get. At scale."
> [TWEET18 #6](https://x.com/naval/status/1002103723848450049)

> "But society doesn't yet know how to create those things because if it did, they wouldn't need you."
> [RICH-08]

> "And then you have to figure out how to scale it. Because if you just build one of it, that's not enough."
> [RICH-08]

On the pattern, from chauffeurs to Uber to scooters: "It's about distributing what rich people used to have
to everybody." [RICH-08]

**The test** (our reading): a wealth idea passes three filters. Society wants it. Society can't yet get it.
You can deliver it at scale.

## Not luck: become the kind of person who makes money

> "A lot of people think making money is about luck. It's not. It's about becoming the kind of person that
> makes money." [RICH-04]

> "In 1,000 parallel universes, you want to be wealthy in 999 of them." [RICH-04]

He describes four kinds of luck, crediting the list to a book via a Marc Andreessen blog post [RICH-04]:

| Kind | His description | Where you control it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Blind luck | "something completely out of my control happened" [RICH-04] | You don't |
| 2 · Luck from hustling | "luck that comes through persistence, hard work, hustle, motion" [RICH-04] | Volume of attempts |
| 3 · Luck from preparation | "you become very good at spotting luck" [RICH-04] | Skill in a field |
| 4 · Luck from your character | "you build a unique character, a unique brand, a unique mindset, where then luck finds you" [RICH-04] | Reputation and brand |

On the fourth: "So, I would characterize that fourth one as you build your character in a certain way and then
your character becomes your destiny." [RICH-05]

> "My personal wealth has not been generated by one big year. It stacks up little bit, chips at a time."
> [RICH-04]

## Protect the freedom you're buying

> "people who are living far below their means enjoy a freedom that people busy upgrading their lifestyles just
> can't fathom." [RICH-07]

> "But ideally you want to make your money in discrete lumps, separated over long periods of time, so that your
> own lifestyle does not have a chance to adapt quickly" [RICH-07]

## No shortcuts, and check who's selling them

> "There are no get rich quick schemes. That's just someone else getting rich off you."
> [TWEET18 #38](https://x.com/naval/status/1002109022420451328)

> "If anyone is giving advice on how to get rich and they're also making money off of it, they should have
> made their money elsewhere." [RICH-36]

## Go at it indirectly

> "if you want to get rich, you don't directly just go for the money." [NAV-indirect] If you build something of
> value with leverage, accountability and specific knowledge, "you're going to make money as a byproduct."
> [NAV-indirect]

The rest of this playbook is that indirect path: specific knowledge (02), leverage (03), accountability and
judgement (04), equity (05), long-term games (06), productizing yourself (07).

**Use it now:** section 1 of `templates/02-leverage-audit.md` lists what you own that earns while you sleep.

**Checks to run:**
1. List everything you own that earns while you sleep. If the list is empty, that's the finding.
2. Which of your goals this year are really status goals? What would you drop if nobody could see the result?
3. What does society want from you that it can't yet get, and could you deliver it a thousand times?
4. Has your lifestyle grown faster than your assets in the last two years?
