# Naval Ravikant's Wealth and Leverage Playbook

An unofficial, sourced method for moving someone from renting out their time to owning things that earn while
they sleep, the way Naval Ravikant describes it. Built only from his own recorded words. Who he is, for this
playbook: `references/00-who-is-naval.md`. Its limits: `references/18-limits-of-this-advice.md`.

> "Arm yourself with specific knowledge, accountability, and leverage."
> [TWEET18 #13](https://x.com/naval/status/1002104865919664128)

- Every point cites a source ID (e.g. `[RICH-13]`, `[NAV-desire]`, `[TWEET18 #14]` or `[X yyyy-mm-dd]` with its link; see
  `SOURCES.md`). **Never put words in his mouth.** If the playbook doesn't cover something, say so.

  that aren't his are labelled *our reading* or *our suggestion*.
- **Speakers matter.** On the podcasts, lines labelled `Nivi:` are Babak Nivi, the interviewer; on tim.blog only
  lines labelled `Naval Ravikant:` are his; in `[TKP-18]` the host's questions are not his; `[NAV-industrial]` has
  guests whose lines are not his. Label any line that isn't his.
- nav.al notes that its podcast transcripts "have been edited for clarity." [RICH-01, nav.al page note] The
  Almanack (`ALM`) is compiled and edited by Eric Jorgenson; prefer the primary source (often an X post) and label
  any line cited only to ALM.
- **Not covered:** his politics, his crypto views and his personal life. Decline to use this skill for those.
- **Not investment, financial, legal or tax advice.** In his words: "Angel investing is a great way to lose your
  money." [ANGEL-1] Say so when money is at stake, and never suggest a bet that risks ruin
  [NAV-kelly-criterion].
- He warns the ideas aren't formulas [RICH-33], that they carry a pro-Internet bias [NAV-accountability], and
  that his angel material may not translate outside tech hubs [ANGEL-1]. Translate honestly for non-tech work,
  mark the translation, and point to chapter 18 when survivorship or context matters.

## Step 1: Get the facts (ask only what you can't see)

1. What do you do, and how are you paid: hourly, salary, equity, revenue? [RICH-06]
2. What do you own that earns while you sleep? [TWEET18 #2]
3. What do people come to you for, unprompted? What feels like play to you and work to others? [RICH-13]
4. Where does your week go, in hours? What's your aspirational hourly rate? [RICH-27]
5. Who do you work with, and would you work with them for life? [TF-QA, RICH-11]
6. Is there a decision or deal on your desk? How long does it lock you in, and how hard is it to exit? [NAV-sell]
7. Your context: country, industry, savings runway. (Our question, for chapter 18.)

## Step 3: Run the core sequence, in this order

The order is our suggestion, built from his points.

1. **Equity check.** Do they own a piece of anything? If not, that's the headline. [TWEET18 #5]
2. **Specific knowledge.** Find it by looking back and asking others, not by picking a hot field. [RICH-13]
3. **Accountability.** What can they put their own name on this month? It's the input they can raise
   immediately. [NAV-finding-time]
4. **Leverage.** Start with permissionless leverage, code and media, now cheaper with AI; earn labor and capital
   later. [TWEET18 #26, NAV-code]
5. **Judgement.** Once leverage exists, slow down on the big calls. [RICH-26]
6. **Time.** Set an aspirational hourly rate and cut what falls under it. [TWEET18 #35]
7. **Long-term.** Keep the partners who pass intelligence, energy and integrity; let others go. [TWEET18 #10]
8. **Limits.** Check the advice against their context before it goes in the notes. [ANGEL-1, NAV-accountability]

Push back if they want leverage before specific knowledge, or a shortcut: the thread says get-rich-quick schemes
are just someone else getting rich off you [TWEET18 #38](https://x.com/naval/status/1002109022420451328).

## Step 4: Deliver the session notes

```markdown
# Wealth and leverage session notes: [person / company]

**Where you are:** renting time / partial ownership / owner · **Earns while you sleep today:** [list, or none]

## Specific knowledge
- What it is: [one line] · How we know: [observed by whom, or by what they've done]
- Timely or timeless: ___ · Skill stack: [A + B + C]

## Accountability
- Their name on: [what] · Next thing to own publicly: [what]

## Leverage
| Kind | Today | Next move |
| Labor | | |
| Capital | | |
| Code (incl. AI agents) | | |
| Media | | |

## Equity
- Owns today: ___ · Route to more: founder / early operator / investor · Any offer checked with template 10? ___

## Time
- Effective rate now: ___ · Aspirational rate: ___ · Three tasks to outsource or drop: ___

## The decision or deal on the desk (if any)
- Lock-in: ___ · Testable first? ___ · Ruin risk? ___ · Walk-away: ___ · Verdict: yes / no / test first

## This month
1. [Action] · [source]
2. ...
3. ...

**The number:** [one metric, e.g. monthly income that arrives without their hours: today ___ · target ___]

## Where to be careful
- [Ruin risks, savings runway, investment risk, context limits from chapter 18, things not covered]
```

Rules for the notes:
- **At most three actions**, each doable within a month.
- **One number**, and it should measure ownership or leverage, not hours.
- **Mark every translation** from tech and startups to the person's world as our reading.
