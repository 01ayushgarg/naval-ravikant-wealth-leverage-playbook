# Sources

Every quote in this repo is from one of the sources below. Before publishing, each quote was checked by script
against the raw text of its source (normalising only quote marks and whitespace), and against the speaker: a
quote tagged with an interview ID must come from Naval's own turns unless it's labelled as someone else's.
Texts were captured on 7 October 2026.

## Labelling rules

- **His words** are in quotation marks with a citation. Nothing else is.
- **Other people's words** (Nivi, Tim Ferriss, people Naval quotes such as @illacertus or Graham via Buffett) are
  labelled with who said them.
- **Our reading** (applications, checks, templates, the ladder table, the arithmetic) is marked as ours.
- **`...`** only joins fragments that are each exact. Quotes are short excerpts, at most two or three
  sentences.
- **Fictional examples** are labelled as fictional.

## Conflicts and caveats

- **Edited transcripts.** nav.al edits its transcripts for clarity, so RICH, ANGEL and NAV podcast quotes are
  close to, not identical with, the audio.
- **The thread text** comes from Wayback Machine snapshots of the old Twitter page, not a live fetch from X. The
  thread's 40 tweets are all dated 31 May 2018 by their IDs; two later replies in the archive are not part of
  it and are not used.
- **Wording differences.** In RICH-09 Nivi reads tweet #9 as *All returns in life*; the archived thread reads
  *All the returns in life*. We quote the archived thread. The Almanack's wording is lightly copy-edited in
  places, which is one reason we cite primary sources first.
- **Lines found only in the Almanack.** *If you can't decide, the answer is no* and *run uphill* (chapter 10)
  and the one-hour-a-day reading line (chapter 09) appear in this corpus only in the Almanack. Its endnote for
  the first points to a 2018 Periscope that we couldn't check. They're labelled `[ALM, compiled by Eric
  Jorgenson]` wherever used.
- **Spelling.** He writes *judgement* in the 2018 tweets and *judgment* on nav.al. Quotes keep his spelling; our
  text uses *judgement*.
- **Dates.** Dates are from nav.al's own date lines and the tweet IDs. One page shows a date a day earlier
  than its index entry (`NAV-do`, 21 vs 22 Jul 2025); we use the index date.
- **Dated numbers.** Startup costs (2009), early-employee equity (2011), angel valuations and fund terms (2009
  to 2020) are as of their date. The playbook says so where they appear.
- **His bias, in his words:** "Obviously, this message is being delivered through the Internet, so it's going
  to have a pro-Internet bias." [NAV-accountability]
