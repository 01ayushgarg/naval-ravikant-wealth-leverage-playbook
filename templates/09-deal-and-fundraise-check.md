# Template 09 · Deal and fundraise check

For a contract, partnership, reseller deal, investor or term sheet: anything that constrains your future.
Source: `references/15-selling-and-dealmaking.md`, `references/16-fundraising-and-term-sheets.md` [NAV-sell,
NAV-short, VH-no-lead, VH-presentation-hacks, VH-startup-principles, NAV-curate-people, TF-097]. The questions are
drawn from his points; the ordering and thresholds are our suggestion. **Not legal advice:** have a lawyer read
any term sheet.
