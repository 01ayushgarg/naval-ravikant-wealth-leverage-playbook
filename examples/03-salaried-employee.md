# Worked example 3: a salaried employee who owns nothing yet

> **Fictional.** Mei, her employer, the offer and every number below are invented to show how the skill works.
> Nothing here is a claim about any real person or company. Quotes from Naval Ravikant are real and cited. Not
> financial, legal or tax advice.

## What she sent

```text
Run Naval's wealth and leverage playbook on my work.

What I do: procurement analyst at a large manufacturer. $95,000 salary, no equity.
What I own: $30,000 in a pension fund. No side income.
What people come to me for: untangling supplier contracts; colleagues forward me the messy ones.
Where my week goes: 45 hours, about 10 of them in status meetings.
The decision: a 30-person startup selling procurement software offers $80,000 plus 0.25%
in options, four-year vesting, one-year cliff, 90-day exercise window.
```

## What the skill returned

### Where you are

Renting out her time, safely. "You're not going to get rich renting out your time."
[TWEET18 #5](https://x.com/naval/status/1002103670400417792) But he doesn't say quit tomorrow: he says you can apply
accountability, leverage and specific knowledge inside your current career [NAV-accountability].

### Specific knowledge

The tell is that colleagues forward her the messy contracts, unprompted. He says specific knowledge is often
"observed by other people who know you well" [RICH-13]. Candidate stack: supplier contracts plus negotiation plus
spreadsheets. → `02-specific-knowledge.md`

### Accountability inside the job

"Accountability is something you can take on immediately." [NAV-finding-time] **Our suggestion:** she volunteers
to own the yearly renegotiation of the 20 largest supplier contracts, with her name on the savings report.

### The offer, through `templates/10-equity-offer-check.md`

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Cash given up | $15,000 a year |
| Options | 0.25% of fully diluted shares (she asks for the count and gets it) |
| After two rounds at 20% each (our default guess) | 0.16% |
| Big win: $300M exit | about $480,000 before tax and strike |
| Modest sale: $40M | about $64,000, less than the $60,000 of salary given up over four years plus risk |
| Zero | $0, the most common outcome |
| Exercise window | 90 days; exercising everything could cost more than a month's salary |

His framing of the route: stock options at a tech company are "a fine way to start." [RICH-06] And from the
founder's side, he says the skills from a founder mentality are part of an employee's pay [NAV-finding-time].

**Our reading:** the equity alone doesn't justify the cut. The case for joining is the learning and the
accountability: she would own procurement for a whole product's customers. She asks for one change, a longer
exercise window, which he'd count as removing an objection [NAV-curate-people].

### Time

Ten hours of status meetings a week. "The only way to do that is to constantly, and ruthlessly, decline
meetings." [RICH-29] At her current job, she replaces two weekly meetings with written updates and gets four hours
back.

### Leverage

Media, permissionless: a monthly post on supplier-contract traps, under her own name. Code: a contract-clause
checker built with a coding agent, used first by her own team. → `03-leverage.md`, `17-ai-era-leverage-and-code.md`

### The decision

Through `templates/06-decision-filter.md`: lock-in four years; ruin risk none (she has six months of costs saved);
excitement high on the work, lukewarm on the pay. Testable? Partly: she spends a paid day with the team first
[RICH-12].

## The session notes (excerpt)

**This month**
1. Ask for the exercise-window change, and the paid day with the team. [RICH-12]
2. Take ownership of the top-20 supplier renegotiation, whichever job she's in. [NAV-finding-time]
3. Publish the first post on contract traps. [TWEET18 #26](https://x.com/naval/status/1002106893265920000)

**The number:** things she owns that earn or compound without her hours (equity, audience, a tool). Today: 0.
Target in 12 months: 2.

**Where to be careful:** don't exercise options with money you can't lose, and check the tax treatment where she
lives before exercising.

## The result (invented)

The startup agreed to a five-year exercise window. She joined. A year later her post list had 1,400 readers and the
clause checker had become a feature in the product, with her name on the release notes.
