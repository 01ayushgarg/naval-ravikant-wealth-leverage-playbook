# Worked example: a wealth and leverage session for "Dana"

> **Fictional.** Dana, her clients, her business and every number below are invented to show how the skill works.
> Nothing here is a claim about any real person or company. Quotes from Naval Ravikant are real and cited.

## What she sent

```text
Run Naval's wealth and leverage playbook on my work.

What I do: freelance analytics for online-store brands. I build sales and inventory dashboards
and explain them to founders on a weekly call.
How I'm paid: $120/hour, about 30 billable hours a week. Roughly $14,000/month.
What I own: nothing that earns without me. Some savings.
Team: two part-time contractors I pay $45/hour.
What people come to me for: why did margin drop last month? Founders say I'm the only
person who explains their numbers in plain English.
Where my week goes: 30 billable, 12 admin and invoicing, 6 sales calls, 4 hours on a
newsletter I started and stopped.
The decision on my desk: a big retailer offers a 12-month retainer at $9,000/month for
20 hours a week. I can't decide.
```

## What the skill returned

### Where you are

You're renting out your time, well. All income stops when you stop. "You're not going to get rich renting out
your time." [TWEET18 #5](https://x.com/naval/status/1002103670400417792) → `05-equity-and-ownership.md`

Asset list that earns while you sleep: empty. That's the finding. → `01-wealth-not-money-or-status.md`

### Specific knowledge

Not dashboards: anyone can be trained to build those. The thing founders name, unprompted, is translating messy
store data into a plain-English answer about margin. Her clients spotted it, which is how he says it usually
shows up: "observed by other people who know you well" [RICH-13].

| Candidate | Trainable in 6 months? | Timely or timeless | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building store dashboards | Yes | Timely | Commodity |
| Explaining margin to non-finance founders | No | Timeless (persuasion plus accounting) | **Core** |
| E-commerce inventory patterns from 40 stores | No | Timely | **Core, combined with the above** |

Her combination (accounting sense, e-commerce data, plain-English persuasion) is the skill stack he describes:
top few percent at three things beats number one at one [RICH-14]. → `02-specific-knowledge.md`

### Leverage audit

| Kind | Today | Move |
|---|---|---|
| Labor | 2 contractors, $45/hour | Hold. He calls labor "the worst form of leverage that you could possibly use" [RICH-21] |
| Capital | None | Not yet. Capital follows demonstrated judgement [TWEET18 #23](https://x.com/naval/status/1002106623639212032) |
| Code | Dashboards rebuilt per client | Turn the margin-explainer into a store app: one product, many stores |
| Media | A dead newsletter | Restart it as a weekly teardown of one anonymised store's margin |

Code and media are the two that need nobody's permission [TWEET18 #26](https://x.com/naval/status/1002106893265920000).
→ `03-leverage.md`

### Accountability

Her work goes out under her clients' names. Nobody outside 9 clients knows what she does. Move: publish under
her own name, and put her name on the app, which is what the thread means by taking business risks under your own
name [TWEET18 #19](https://x.com/naval/status/1002105652792066048). → `04-accountability-and-judgement.md`

### Hourly rate and time

| | Now |
|---|---|
| Billed rate | $120/hour |
| Effective rate (income ÷ all 52 working hours) | about $62/hour |
| Aspirational rate set in the session | $600/hour |

Task audit at $600/hour (invented numbers):

| Task | Hours/week | Cost at $600/hour | Outsource cost | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invoicing and admin | 12 | $7,200 | Bookkeeper, about $400/week | **Outsource** |
| Sales calls for one-off projects | 6 | $3,600 | n/a | **Cut to 2**; sell retainers and the app instead |
| Newsletter | 4 → 6 | $3,600 | n/a | **Keep**: it's the media leverage |

The thread's rule: outsource any task that costs less than your hourly rate
[TWEET18 #35](https://x.com/naval/status/1002108599399661568). → `08-time-and-the-hourly-rate.md`

### The decision: the $9,000/month retainer

Run through `templates/06-decision-filter.md`:

| Filter | Answer |
|---|---|
| Lock-in | 12 months, 20 hours a week: two thirds of her productive time |
| Can it be tested cheaply? | Yes: a 6-week paid pilot. "At least do the first step, and the second step, and the third, and then decide." [RICH-12] |
| Ruin risk? | None |
| Excited? | No. It's more rented time at about $104/hour |
| Does it build anything she owns? | No |

She couldn't decide. His checkable rule for that is "So, when in doubt, just say no." [VH-angel] **Our
reading:** say no to the 12-month lock-in and, following his test-by-doing rule [RICH-12], yes to a pilot, which
keeps the option open while the evidence comes in. → `10-decisions.md`

### Long-term partner check

One contractor, Sam (also fictional), has worked with her for three years, has shipped on every deadline, and
once flagged his own billing error in her favour. Integrity seen, not claimed [RICH-11]. Offer (our suggestion): a 20% stake in
the app, with vesting, instead of hourly pay for that work. → `06-long-term-games-and-partners.md`,
`templates/04-long-term-partner-check.md`

### Productize yourself

"Productize has specific knowledge and leverage. Yourself has uniqueness and accountability." [RICH-37]

Her product: the margin-explainer app, sold to the stores she already knows, with the newsletter as the sales
channel. She writes better than she sells on calls, which is his suggested route for builders [RICH-15].
→ `templates/05-productize-yourself-plan.md`

## Her session notes (excerpt)

**Where you are:** renting time, no equity. **Your specific knowledge:** explaining e-commerce margin in plain
English. **Leverage to add next:** code (the app) and media (the teardown).

**This month**
1. Hire a bookkeeper; free 12 hours a week. [RICH-27]
2. Ship the teardown every Thursday under her own name. [TWEET18 #19](https://x.com/naval/status/1002105652792066048)
3. Offer the retailer a 6-week pilot, not 12 months. [RICH-12]

**The number:** monthly revenue that arrives without her hours. Today: $0. Target in 12 months: $5,000.

**Where to be careful:** her income drops while the app is built. Keep six months of costs in savings and avoid
anything that risks ruin [NAV-kelly-criterion].

## The result (invented)

Six months later: the pilot turned into a smaller 8-hour retainer; the newsletter reached 2,100 store owners; the
app had 64 paying stores at $49/month ($3,136 a month, earning while she sleeps). Billable hours fell from 30 to
16. The next constraint was support, which she gave to Sam along with more equity.
